          How it works, and the rules

          Plain-English summary of the member terms. Placeholder wording, to be checked by a solicitor before launch.

          Your money

          • Money you add is pre-payment for food, not an investment. It earns no interest or return.
          • You decide which items your money is earmarked for. When orders close, the network buys at the best price its combined pot reaches, and your share is committed.
          • Anything that isn't needed stays on the item as carry-over. You can keep it there, move it to another item, or move it to unassigned and withdraw it to your card.
          • If an item doesn't reach even its smallest bulk quantity, nothing is bought and all the money on it carries over.

          Collecting

          • Show your QR code at your pot's hub during your collection slot. Scanning it hands over your parcel and completes the purchase.
          • If you don't collect by the end of the slot, we'll message you and hold chilled items until 4pm where we can. After that, uncollected food is donated to a local food bank or community fridge, and the money isn't refunded, because the food was bought for you and can't be resold.
          • This doesn't affect your legal rights if the food was faulty or not as described.

          Someone collecting for you

          • You can nominate someone in your pot. It only takes effect when they accept.
          • Once their code is scanned, the parcel counts as handed to you. After that, loss, damage or mix-ups between you and them aren't our responsibility.
          • We remain responsible for the food being safe and as described when it left us.

          Delivery

          • Where available, delivery costs a flat fee per cycle, taken from your unassigned credit when orders close. If there's not enough, you'll collect instead.

          Group chat

          • Opt-in, per pot. Be respectful and no selling. We remove illegal or abusive content and can suspend accounts.
