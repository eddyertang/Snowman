            Member rules

            The short version. Placeholder wording, to be checked by a solicitor before launch.

            Your money

            • Money you add is payment in advance for food, not an investment. It doesn't earn interest.
            • When you choose an item, we set aside today's price. You'll never pay more than that.
            • When orders close, all pots buy together at the best price we reach. Anything left over stays on that item for next week. You can move it, or send it back to your card any time.
            • If an item doesn't reach even its smallest bulk amount, nothing is bought and your money stays put.

            Collecting

            • Show your code at your hub during your pot's slot. Scanning it hands over your food and completes the purchase.
            • If you don't collect, we'll message you and keep chilled food until 4pm where we can. After that it goes to a local food bank and can't be refunded, because it was bought for you.
            • This doesn't affect your rights if food is faulty or not as described.

            Someone collecting for you

            • You can ask someone in your pot. It only counts once they say yes.
            • Once their code is scanned, your food counts as handed to you. After that, anything that happens between you and them isn't our responsibility.
            • We're still responsible for the food being safe and as described when it left us.

            Delivery and group chat

            • Delivery, where available, is a flat fee each week, taken from your wallet when orders close. If there isn't enough in your wallet, you'll collect instead.
            • The group is opt-in. Be respectful and don't sell things. We remove illegal or abusive posts.

            Volunteer scanner

            On the real app this uses the phone camera. For the demo, tap a member or paste a code.
