How a cycle works

  1. Join a local pot

    Your postcode puts you in a pot of up to 120 households near one hub. When a pot fills, a new one opens.

  2. Put money on what you want

    Top up, then earmark money to items and set the most you'd use. You see the live price and your saving as the pot grows.

  3. We buy at the best tier

    Thursday 8pm, all pots buy together at the cheapest bulk price the money reaches. Anything you didn't need stays on the item as credit.

  4. Scan and collect

    Saturday, show your QR code at the hub. Or let someone in your pot collect for you, or pay for delivery.

Where every £10 goes

We add about 15% to the farm-gate price and publish exactly what it pays for. Common Crop is set up as a Community Interest Company, so surplus goes back into lower prices and the hubs.
