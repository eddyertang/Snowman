How it works

  1. Join your local pot

    Your postcode puts you with up to 120 households near one hub. Everyone's orders are combined across the county.

  2. Pick what you want

    Choose how many of each. The more neighbours join a buy, the lower the price, and you'll never pay more than you've set aside.

  3. Grab it on Saturday

    Show your code at the hub. Or ask a neighbour to pick yours up, or get it delivered.

Anything you don't use stays in your wallet for next week, or you can take it back out.

Meet the farmers

Every item comes from a named farm, usually less than 20 miles away. Farmers are paid within 7 days.

Where your £10 goes

We add about 15% to what the farm charges, and we're open about where it goes. We're a Community Interest Company, so any surplus goes back into lower prices.

    Questions

    Is it really cheaper than the supermarket?

    For most things, yes. We compare like-for-like: British free-range eggs with British free-range eggs, grass-fed beef with grass-fed beef, using the supermarket's best-value pack size. Tap "How we compared" on any item to see the sums.

    What if the price changes after I order?

    You choose how many you want and we set aside today's price, so you'll never pay more than that. If more neighbours join and the price drops, the difference stays in your wallet. If people drop out and it goes up, you might get one fewer, and we'll tell you before Saturday.

    What happens to money I don't use?

    It stays in your wallet. Use it next week, put it on something else, or send it back to your card whenever you like.

    I can't make it on Saturday.

    Ask someone in your pot to collect for you (they have to say yes), or choose delivery if it's available near you. Food that isn't collected goes to a local food bank, and we can't refund it.

    Where does the food come from?

    Farms across Nottinghamshire. Each item shows the farm and how far away it is. Meat is butchered and labelled by licensed butchers, and eggs are graded and stamped.

    Be one of the first 200

    Founding neighbours choose which farms and hubs we start with, and get first dibs on meat shares.

    Farmer or grower?

    Get paid in 7 days, sell your gluts and grade-outs, and deliver to one place. Say hello and we'll call you.
