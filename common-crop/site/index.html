How a cycle works

  1. Pledge

    Orders open Friday. Pledge shares in crowd-buys or add standing items. Your card is saved, not charged.

  2. Target met, we order

    Thursday 8pm the window closes. Crowd-buys that hit their target become one order per farm. Only then are you charged.

  3. Farms deliver to the hub

    One drop per farm, straight to the hub. Chilled goods go in the fridges and get temperature-logged.

  4. You collect

    Saturday morning, bring a bag. Anything unclaimed by 1pm is kept chilled and you'll get a message.

Where every £10 goes

We add about 15% to the farm-gate price and publish exactly what it pays for. Common Crop is set up as a Community Interest Company, so surplus goes back into lower prices and the hubs.
